The brief

A ride-hailing platform starting from nothing — no design language, no component library, no team. Three surfaces had to ship in lockstep: an app for riders, an app for drivers, and a console for operators. They needed to feel like one product, scale city by city, and be built fast.

I owned design end to end, and the marketing that put it on the road.

What I did

  • Design system from scratch. Type, color, spacing, iconography, and components — one source of truth across all three products.
  • Rider app. Booking, tracking, payments and support — designed for first-time users in new cities.
  • Driver app. Earnings, navigation and trip flow, built for drivers using it all day on low-end devices.
  • Operator console. The back office that kept the network running.
  • Built the team. Hired and led the design and development team from the ground up.
  • Took it to market. Launched city by city with on-ground events and press.
