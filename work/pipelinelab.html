The brief

The PipelineLab builds scalable outbound systems for B2B teams — ICP, list-building, deliverability, multichannel sequences — and delivers consistent pipeline. Work here is part of the ₹6 Cr+ in B2B revenue I've generated.

So the studio's own brand had to do what it sells: be sharp, clear, and convert. I built all of it.

What I did

  • Identity. A brand for a GTM studio — technical, direct, no fluff. The same precision the outbound work runs on.
  • Website. Designed and shipped the site — the offer, the proof, and the way in.
  • Explainer film. Motion that explains how the pipeline gets built, fast.

I don't just advise on go-to-market. I run it, brand it, and ship it — the studio is the proof.

How the engine works

  • ICP & lists. Define who to reach, then build clean, targeted lists.
  • Deliverability. Infrastructure that lands in the inbox, not spam.
  • Multichannel sequences. Outreach that compounds across channels.
  • Pipeline. Consistent, measurable meetings booked.
